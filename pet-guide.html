06. Myna

Affinity items providing temporary Attack or Defense bonuses. Scales with Affinity level and Pet Grade.

Exchange Rough Myna Orbs for Myna. Costs Red Mushrooms based on orb quality.

07. Trait

Trait stats can be trained ~1300 points above max growth.

Example: Arborik 7130 Weaken Res + Weaken Trait → 8340.

Release pets to get Jades matching their Trait. Use Body Strengthen Potion to reroll Traits (50 Sunny Jade).

08. Pet Age

If Age reaches max, the pet dies. Buy Age-reducing or Life-increasing items at the Pet Master Shop.

09. Pet Growth and Grade

Higher Growth = Higher Grade. Grade allows appearance Evolution. Use Pet Jade or Celebeans to train. Failures yield minimal points; success chances drop at high growth.

Reaching Grade 12 and Grade 18 are the most critical milestones. These grades unlock the ability to use Evolution Mushrooms to transform your pet's physical form.

11. Skill Jades

Replace/Upgrade skills with Skill Jades. Advanced Jades cost History of Domestication (Item Exchange).

12. Pet Soul, Evolution and Mutation

Pet Souls are purchased from the Marketplace, and will change your pet's appearance to look like a different pet.

Evolution

Once your pet has reached the appropriate grades, use Blue Mushrooms (Evolution at Grade 12) or Colorful Mushrooms (Evolution at Grade 18) to change your pet's appearance.

Mutation

Use Gem Mushrooms at Grade 12 or Grade 18 to trigger mutations for alternate skins.

You can also use Red Mushrooms to revert the effects of the other mushrooms. Buy these from the Pet Master's Pet Shop and Item Exchange.

13. Aptitudes

Depending on their type, pets are proficient in different aptitudes. A random value will be assigned to the respective aptitude and these change when using a Menggu Relic.

Pet Gear

Consisting of the Pet Workshop and Gear Fortification.

Pet Workshop

The Workshop uses items gathered by pets while foraging to craft Pet Gear and remedies, including Mirage Candy that grants EXP to your pets. It is the only way to craft the best gear, requiring high Tech Stats and Development.

Craft Levels

  • Level 1: Basic Pet Gear.
  • Level 2: +1 and +2 Pet Gear.
  • Tech Stats: Automatically added by Level 60+ pets (marked with *).

Foraging Recipes
